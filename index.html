Find your rhythm

One real wheel. A 90-second run. Jump hurdles, duck under branches, and collect cheese.

Jump
Space, click, or tap the track. The Jump button works too.
Duck
Hold ↓ or swipe down on the track. Tap Duck for a short slide.
Pause
Press P or Escape, or use the pause button. Leaving the tab pauses your run.

The wheel is the engine

In RUN, speed and beat timing follow the live RPM, up to 120. In NAP (2 RPM or less), the track slows, obstacles freeze, and Dream Cheese drifts toward you. Jump to catch the high pieces.

Catch a Perfect Squeek

Collect cheese during the ring’s 120 ms beat flash: your combo goes up by one and the cheese earns 50 points instead of 10. Dream Cheese earns 30. Distance and cheese points use your current combo. Bumps reset it to ×1; the run continues.

Live means live

The public Squeekchain status API updates every 5 seconds. The latest reported SqueekCore round seeds the track, so the same round gives everyone the same layout. After three failed readings, the game switches to labelled SIM practice. Select LIVE to reconnect.

SIM uses a built-in wheel cycle and its own practice seed. Live and practice records stay separate in this browser. No account or purchase is needed.
